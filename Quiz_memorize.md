## Section Quiz — Literals & Number Systems

**Q1.** What types of literals are `"Hello "`, `"007"`?  
- ✅ Both are **string literals**.

**Q2.** What types of literals are `"1.5"`, `2.0`, `528`, `False`?  
- `"1.5"` → string literal  
- `2.0` → float literal  
- `528` → integer literal  
- `False` → boolean literal  

**Q3.** What is the decimal value of binary `1011`?  
- ✅ `11` (since \(2^0 + 2^1 + 2^3 = 11\))
